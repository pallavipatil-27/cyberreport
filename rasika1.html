Cyber Crime Awareness

Learn about common cyber threats and how they affect users.

🎣

Phishing

Fraudulent emails, messages or websites designed to steal passwords and personal information.

💳

Online Payment Fraud

Scammers use fake payment requests, links or impersonation to steal money.

📱

Social Media Fraud

Fake profiles and suspicious messages target social media users.

🔐

Password Attacks

Attempts to obtain passwords and gain unauthorized access to accounts.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software designed to damage systems or steal information.

👤

Identity Theft

Unauthorized use of someone's personal information for fraudulent activities.

🛒

Online Shopping Fraud

Fake shopping websites, products or payment schemes designed to deceive customers.

📧

Email Scams

Fraudulent emails that trick users into revealing information or sending money.

Cyber Safety Tips

Follow these basic practices to stay safer online.

🔐 Use strong passwords: Use long, unique passwords for important accounts.
🚫 Never share OTPs: Never share confidential OTPs or passwords.
🔗 Check links: Avoid suspicious links received through unexpected messages.
📱 Enable two-factor authentication: Add an additional security layer to your accounts.
🌐 Verify websites: Check the website address before entering personal or payment information.
🔄 Keep software updated: Install security updates for your devices and applications.
